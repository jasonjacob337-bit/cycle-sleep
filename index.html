How the three sources fit together

Luteal phase→Skin temp ↑ · resting HR ↑ · HRV ↓→Sleep: mostly unchanged

Three charts, three answers

A · Does the body change with phase?
Skin temp: higher in luteal (p<0.001)
Resting HR: higher in luteal (p<0.03)
HRV: tends to be lower
Yes. Clear, repeatable shifts at night. (Alzueta et al., 2022)
B · Does sleep change with phase?
≈0
Sleep efficiency, duration, latency, quality
No significant change in healthy women (n = 116). (Alzueta et al., 2024)
C · What does less sleep do?
RHR +1.2%
HRV −2.8%
Per ~10% less sleep, in every phase. (Gonzalez et al., 2026)
